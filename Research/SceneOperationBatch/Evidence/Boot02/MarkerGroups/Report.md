# Marker groups: Boot02 manual trial

The [selected telemetry](SelectedTelemetry.txt) records one manual T-46 trial in the scene-operations fixture. It supports [Test track-relative placement and group subscription changes](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/issues/24). The distant moved placement was not visibly observed. Automatic application and editor or ordinary-save reload were not tested in this trial.

The baseline had marker `Mdow` on `Stvw` in group `sy-db`, with one matching registration, one native group subscription and an active target. Both groups were enabled. The target offset was `(3.20, 0, 0)`, angle 180 degrees, and terrain snapping was enabled. The probe rebound it to the T-93 location on `Sn9f` in `na-an` and applied offset `(2, 0, 1)`, angle 15 degrees, with terrain snapping disabled. Both the first apply and a repeat returned true, logged the same target pose to displayed precision, and recorded one matching `Sn9f` registration and one native subscription observing `na-an`. This is transform telemetry, not visual confirmation of the new placement.

After rebind, toggling `sy-db` left the target active with one registration; toggling `na-an` off made it inactive with no registration, and toggling it on restored activity and its matching registration. These are observations of this marker during this trial, not a guarantee for other consumers or group members.

The probe's explicit-disable callback fired on new-group activation and left the target inactive with no registration. On repeat application while explicit disable was set, the probe first logged the target active with one registration, then logged it inactive with no registration after reinstalling the callback and reapplying disable. The log therefore establishes the final disabled state after that repeat, but not uninterrupted disable. It does not record another group off/on after the repeat, so it does not establish callback precedence through a subsequent activation after that repeat.

Restoration logged successful original placement configuration, original `Stvw` binding, one matching registration, one native subscription observing `sy-db`, no probe callback, both groups enabled, and the baseline root pose to displayed precision. The probe then toggled both groups: `sy-db` controlled target activity and registration, while `na-an` did not disable it. The final matching registration check passed. The probe compares registration object, ID, bucket and location fields; these checks and the displayed pose do not establish bitwise equality, all dependent state, or restoration of every other group member. Its explicit-disable callback was absent from the restored snapshot. No separate post-restoration visual claim follows from the log.
