# Crossing pad result

The [Boot05 trial](../Evidence/Boot05/CrossingPad/README.md) repeats the [Boot02](../Evidence/Boot02/CrossingPad/README.md) same-segment Mg74 rebind with a clear view of both crossings. Rebinding by one metre left the sampled mesh and culling sphere unchanged during the observation. Native `Generate` changed both, and the front pad visibly shifted along the track relative to the road while the rear pad appeared unchanged. Restoring the binding alone retained the displaced pad. A second Generate returned its visible alignment and the probe's sampled mesh and sphere matched baseline. The camera moved slightly after that Generate during verification.

This is a rebind-only result for one pad and one observation window. The probe did not apply track-relative placement. Its signature does not cover complete geometry or every renderer property, and the trial did not establish reload persistence or behavior for other pads. The [preparation report](README.md) describes the static paths and safeguards.
