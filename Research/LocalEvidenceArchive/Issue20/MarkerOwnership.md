Disabled renderers in this snapshot do not establish that the sign is permanently invisible or why the culler disabled them.

Read-only Mono.Cecil inspection of the hash-matched installed assembly found `TrackMarker.OnEnable` resolves its location and registers its ID, type and object in `Graph.AddNamedLocation`; `OnDisable` calls `RemoveNamedLocation`. `TrackRelativePosition.Awake` obtains the colocated marker. Its `TryApply` reads the marker position and uses the target, offset, angle and terrain-snap fields to move the target; the group-following branch can also change subscriptions. `RendererCuller.Awake` collects child renderers and manipulates their enabled state. These are method paths and captured field links, not a test that either candidate was moved or that culling was restored. The [marker consumer note](../Issue18/MarkerConsumers.md) records the separately inspected graph query paths.

## Ownership and decision consequences

The two captured roots are members of the loaded map-manager scene, with the listed components and no map-object spawner component on their captured ancestors or subtrees. Combined with the [typed editor and serializer audit](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/8d3bfc0/Research/NativeCapabilityAudit.md), this supports treating these instances as retained scene candidates for identity and authoring analysis. It does not prove their original asset serialization provenance, rule out an indirect DTO association, or establish a native map-mod save route. A component type or matching marker ID alone is not proof of a native DTO owner.

For issue 9, use the scene identity, full path, marker ID, type and binding together and validate uniqueness and registration per load. For issue 8, the captured retained roots and typed serializer boundary leave inherited-scene authoring unestablished. For issue 18, both candidates have a concrete marker and target relationship suitable for a separately scoped applicability investigation. Capture, registration and field references do not prove safe rebind, movement, group behavior, restoration, persistence, or yard and station gameplay effects. Mkwd's culler adds a distinct renderer-state dependency; Mi4f's recorded hierarchy does not contain that component. No claim of absent world consumers follows from the bounded scan.

## Probe and limits

The independently authored `CandidateProbe/` uses the lifecycle and explicit GUI capture pattern in [ApplicableProbe](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/b41a6dda255accf2de1949414b35ea37f4b8d63c/Research/ApplicableProbe/ApplicableProbe.cs). It reads marker fields, ancestor and subtree components, track-relative target subtrees, graph registrations and direct loaded-scene field references. Target subtrees are capped at 256 nodes with truncation logged. It does not mutate the scene or save. The capture is a point-in-time observation; it does not establish behavior across reloads or identify consumers outside that scan.

The successful build used the installed game assemblies and emitted no warnings.
