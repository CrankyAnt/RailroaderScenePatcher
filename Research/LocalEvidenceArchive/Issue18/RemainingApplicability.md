## Distinct consumers and ownership

| Original candidate | Evidence and applicable boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Whistle, speed, yard-limit, station-sign markers | `Track.TrackMarker.OnEnable` resolves its own location and registers its ID, type and object in `Track.Graph.AddNamedLocation`. This is shared registration, not proof that every category has the same downstream consumer. The original [batch](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/4c00445b048adccb6920c238116d1f7e1e915db9/Research/Scope/Tests/07-Inventory/Result.md) identifies broken speed Mwp1, yard-limit Mkwd, whistle Mm8w and station-sign Mi4f. Their physical labels do not establish marker enum or behavior. Inspect each instance's actual enum, attached components, registered object and downstream graph queries. The speed, yard and station operational consumers remain unproven; marker registration or a moved Transform cannot establish their equivalence. |
| Crossing pads | The original identified pad Mg74 has `TrackMarker`, `TrackRelativePosition` and `TrackCurveMeshBuilder` ([scene targets](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/4c00445b048adccb6920c238116d1f7e1e915db9/Research/Scope/SceneBatchTargets.md)). `TrackCurveMeshBuilder.GetCurves` finds a colocated marker if its cached field is empty, reads its location and uses half its width to derive graph curves; invalid marker or null segment returns an empty list. `BezierCurveMesh.CurveMeshBuilderBase.OnEnable` calls `Generate`, whose implementation destroys existing container children and generates mesh renderers from those curves, with a culling token. This is a separate geometry and culling consumer. Whether rebinding automatically regenerates an already-enabled pad, whether colliders or other equipment are affected, and exact recovery behavior are unproven. Inspect mesh and culling before and after rebind, explicit regeneration or lifecycle transition, restoration and reload. Do not infer that moving a root repairs its mesh. |
| Protected crossing | The named controller owns two circuit sets and six child spans, with separate island and approach consumers. The native audit and operations matrix identify them. Marker, span endpoints and physical equipment are independent checks; no crossing operation has been demonstrated. |
| Industry and passenger spans | `IndustryDataLoader.CreatePassengerStop` constructs a `PassengerStop`, resolves `PassengerStopDTO.trackSpans` through the supplied span lookup and assigns `Spans`. `PassengerStop.FindCars` queries `TrainController.CarsOnSpan` for those spans and filters stopped passenger cars. These facts identify a concrete map-data passenger consumer, not an independent scene-owned stop. The original I05 test's Wilmot station span is collateral native OpsDTO data, not a scene-only finding. `StationAgent` is a scene-capable MonoBehaviour with `passengerStopId`; `ResolveReferences` looks up a stop in `OpsController.AllPassengerStops`. This is a distinct scene-to-ops identifier link, but no affected instance or repair need was established. Inspect actual owner, identifier and native data before assigning any scene operation. Source: installed `Railroader.dll`, named methods and DTO fields; [inventory result](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/4c00445b048adccb6920c238116d1f7e1e915db9/Research/Scope/Tests/07-Inventory/Result.md). |
| Built-in anchors | The Sox water-column case was repaired through map JSON ([scope inventory](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/4c00445b048adccb6920c238116d1f7e1e915db9/Research/Scope/ScopeInventory.md)). It establishes the owner for that case only. `Game.Builtins.BuiltinBuilder.DisableUnresolvedTrackMarkers` is another concrete code pointer, not proof that all anchors are native or that disabling a marker preserves its consumers. An unidentified scene anchor remains an empirical unknown. |
| Independent location board | Its inspected hierarchy has no marker or track-relative component. Root pose and activity are applicable to that board; mask, culling and floating-origin effects remain open. Do not generalize its hierarchy to other boards. See the operations matrix. |
| Mileposts | Marker binding and independent mileage-origin relationships remain distinct. T-46/T-47 identifies an origin/dependent candidate, not proven cached-origin resolution or restoration. See the operations matrix. |
| Bridge and related construction scenery | The Ela bridge root and construction scenery are separate identified roots. Pose and activity observations do not establish collision contact, construction or group behavior. See the operations matrix. |
| Group-following visuals and progression | Track-relative target activity can follow a group; T-93 is an identified candidate. Progression group lists are separate consumers. Callback ordering, disable precedence and affected progression remain untested. See the operations matrix. |
| Signals, CTC and interlocking | Explicitly excluded from the recorded V1 boundary, but retained here as original inventory accounting. CTC child span endpoints and auto-signal block object references are separate scene relationships; neither establishes a reproduced repair. See the reference families and native audit. |

The native editor and player-sign routes, including save ownership of the observed ordinary-save sign, are recorded in the operations matrix. They do not establish editing an existing retained scene marker. For every unresolved family, the minimum next evidence is a concrete instance and its owner, actual binding and consumer, followed by an independently observed change and restoration where an operation applies. The table does not treat untested families as excluded.
