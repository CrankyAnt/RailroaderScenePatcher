# Support boundary evidence gaps

Fact lookup for map issue 3 and decision issue 7. This records existing research, not new game tests. The inventory documents describe Railroader 0.6.2 and mutable official documentation checked on 2026-11-07. Recheck current behavior before relying on a categorical editor capability claim.

## Existing evidence

- [Inventory](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/4c00445b048adccb6920c238116d1f7e1e915db9/Research/README.md) observed missing references for yard limit, station, speed, milepost, crossing pad and whistle markers. Physical pose and logical track reference can differ. Some location boards have no marker.
- [Editor matrix](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/4c00445b048adccb6920c238116d1f7e1e915db9/Research/Scope/EditorTestMatrix.md) and [candidate families](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/4c00445b048adccb6920c238116d1f7e1e915db9/Research/Scope/CandidateFamilies.md) propose tests; they do not report completion. Their earlier family exclusions are not the current boundary.
- [Editor hooks](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/research/issue-4-editor-authoring-hooks/Research/EditorAuthoringHooks.md) inspected possible internal connections, without runtime validation at that stage. [Editor diagnostics](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/research/issue-14-editor-diagnostics/Research/EditorDiagnostics.md) demonstrated a bounded separate diagnostic UI and native node operations, not a full native editor inventory. Neither establishes the speed-sign context menu. [Ownership readiness](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/research/issue-15-ownership-readiness/Research/OwnershipReadiness.md) concerns map-pack ownership and scene readiness, not ownership of each object.
- [Operations](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/3db820db8e34b5dc98b6df4c4ffa376963bfa440/Research/SceneOperations.md) records a single marker rebind and direct pose and explicit restoration in an editor fixture, and limited sandbox active-state observations. Reload after restoration does not establish persistence or automatic reapplication. Milepost, bridge and crossing operations were untested.
- [Lifecycle](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/0f8d3fb69cc362c5a56a22910bdb5a93aa75acc3/Research/SceneLifecycle.md) reports static group callbacks that may reactivate targets and milepost origin/cache relationships. [Reference families](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/4c00445b048adccb6920c238116d1f7e1e915db9/Research/Scope/GameReferenceFamilies.md) distinguishes native map-data OpsDTO spans from scene components and identifies progression group references, without a demonstrated progression failure.

## Questions left open

1. For each original inventory family except CTC, signals and interlocking, identify representative instances and their ownership, editable fields and dependencies. Establish which native editor operations work, including sign context menus, selection, saved fields and reload. Separate native map-data capabilities from retained scene-object capabilities.
2. Identify applicable pose, rotation, activity, marker location, segment, end, distance, track node, span endpoints and other object-specific bindings. Test effects on registrations and consumers, and capture sufficient original state for restoration. Do not infer support from type names alone.
3. Identify a real protected crossing controller and its circuit ownership. Test approach and island references, trains in both directions, occupancy, gates, lights and bell, separately from visual pose and disable behavior.
4. Test explicit patch disable against track-group callbacks, including old and new group subscriptions. Explicit disable must override group activation; otherwise retain existing group behavior. Establish repeat application, removal, reload and restoration of original pose, activity, bindings, registration and relevant dependent state.
5. For a milepost and its origin and same-prefix dependents, establish original placement and bindings and whether restoration restores their dependent behavior. Mileage value editing is out of scope, as are mesh, model and livery editing.
6. Inspect the currently available progression mod API and actual group dependencies. Establish effects and possible repair without assuming a future API.

Existing issues 4, 5, 14, 15 and 16 overlap individual mechanisms or examples but do not close this native-capability and object-ownership audit. The inventory's earlier editor limitations and candidate exclusions are dated evidence, not a current support decision.
