# Applicable operations continuation assessment

This static reassessment serves [applicable scene-object operations](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/issues/18) and the decisions in [first usable product map](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/issues/3). No game test was performed for this assessment. The retained experiments use Railroader 0.6.2 experimental build 26335745 and the assembly hash recorded in [ApplicableSceneOperations.md](ApplicableSceneOperations.md); that hash does not identify all scene assets. References to other research branches below use immutable report URLs. Their observations do not establish a general runtime contract.

## Order and session boundary

First inspect the retained component paths and event ordering. Then use the existing marker fixture to resolve placement, group subscriptions and readiness together, keeping each snapshot and restoration independently verifiable. Follow with the board, milepost, crossing-pad and bridge cases, each only after its affected state is captured. These tests answer different consumer paths and cannot be replaced by one generic transform test. Warning timing can share the broken-marker fixture but needs its own ordered trace.

The valid-but-wrong crossing route is a concrete independent continuation if it cannot fit in the same 100K-token session. Its exact question is the first row: on the named crossing and a disposable rerouted track, which explicit membership or binding change transfers approach and island detection, preserves intended equipment response, and restores the original affected state in both directions? It consumes the additional crossing detection and crossing equipment research rather than repeating construction feasibility. It must block the crossing circuit boundary and affected authoring experience, patch document and target identity, and invalid-patch and conflict behavior decisions before the applicable scene-object operations investigation can close with that work outstanding. No continuation is created by this assessment.

[Additional crossing equipment](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/9c0403e487c98a0ac422ed67fa103e19d9e94be3/Research/CrossingEquipment.md) tested a retained combined lamp/gate and separately bound bell, including removal and fresh-world reconstruction. Its culler did not include the clone, its sound was not isolated by listening, and its asset lifetime depended on the original. The [additional crossing detection report](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/9495abf1fcef5f3c129a6ebd63a0af8a3c62604a/Research/AdditionalCrossingDetection.md) also records a base-map first-activation gate anomaly. Account for these observations when interpreting physical behavior; neither proves a reroute repair or universal cleanup.

Cross-version selectors, arbitrary origin shifts, all collector implementations, all crossing layouts and exhaustive release tests are not established. They need further research only when a selected compatibility or failure guarantee requires them. The [native capability audit](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/blob/8d3bfc0005d15e8b0404fc584479bd60f385c4fd/Research/NativeCapabilityAudit.md) and the candidate ownership research already supply native sign routes and candidate ownership; do not repeat them. Native operations data and the historical passenger warning do not provide a current scene-mutation fixture.
