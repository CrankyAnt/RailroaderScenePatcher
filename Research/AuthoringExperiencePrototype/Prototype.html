Crossing authoring interaction prototype

Can a modder choose an ambiguous approach track in the scene, inspect and navigate without losing the choice, and complete one grouped edit? This is a proposed custom interaction, not an editor extension or proof of a supported integration. Native right-drag camera movement cancels an armed pick, and changing native selection clears its target. This demo instead keeps a separate custom draft while navigating or inspecting. Its unsaved indicator compares scene snapshots, a simplification that does not claim to reproduce native change counting.

Scene

Purple marks retained original coverage T-001. Green marks added coverage, blue marks a pending candidate, and blue dashes mark both. Click a node or segment to inspect it. Navigation remains available while choosing.

Actions

Add track coverage is a provisional operation. It binds and registers the selected track together as one edit.

Equipment examples: move existing equipment, or add validated equipment and delete it. Restore removes this mod's existing-equipment modification and reveals the inherited baseline as a new edit. It does not assume the baseline is the original game.
