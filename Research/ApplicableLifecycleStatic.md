# Lifecycle and placement: static inspection

Supporting evidence for [issue 18](https://github.com/CrankyAnt/RailroaderScenePatcher/issues/18). Read-only inspection on 8 November 2026 used installed Railroader 0.6.2 experimental `Railroader_Data/Managed/Railroader.dll`, SHA-256 `8E9ACFD583C1DA79655C970773D42F2179E999F62DC36A3C6BC3A7B94C27A91E`, verified with `Get-FileHash`. The assembly was inspected with local ilspycmd 10.1.1 and Mono.Cecil reference searches. Method names below identify the primary source. No game execution was part of this inspection. Public visibility does not establish a supported modding contract. Findings are conditional on this build and do not establish every Unity activation order or loader path.

## Load sequence and warning

`UI.Menu.GlobalGameManager._Launch` publishes `Game.Events.MapWillLoadEvent` before unloading the main menu and before connecting the client. In `HandleClientDidReceiveSnapshot`, when the map differs or no map is current, `LoadMap` is awaited before `PopulateFromRemoteSnapshot` and `IMainScriptLifecycle.ExecuteMainScriptsOnWorldLoad`; `AssetPackManager.ExecuteMainScriptsOnWorldLoad` invokes managed `ModAssemblyManager.WorldLoad`, which invokes `IWorldLifecycle.OnWorldLoad`. `LoadMap` loads GameUI and then its scene list, including MapCore and the map scene. Thus the early event does not itself provide a demonstrated loaded target or destination graph; the conditional same-map path and other entry paths require separate verification. `Game.Events.EventBus.Subscribe<T>(UnityEngine.Object, Action<T>)` and its disposable `Subscribe<T>(Action<T>)` overload are available for instrumentation. Registration before publication is itself a condition to test.

`Game.MapLoader.Awake` loads map data, and `LoadMapData` assigns `Track.Graph.Shared` after `MapDataLoader.Load`. `MapLoader.LoadUI` yields, assigns the graph to `TrainController.Shared`, creates `MapObjectManager`, initializes turntables and eventually publishes `MapDidLoadEvent` after another yield. This is not proof that the scene marker has not already enabled: `TrackMarker.OnEnable` resolves its `Location` and either registers it with `Graph.AddNamedLocation` or immediately issues the invalid-location diagnostic. `MapWillLoadEvent` is earlier but cannot be assumed to have both needed objects, and neither `OnModLoad` nor `MapDidLoadEvent` is demonstrated to provide a usable pre-warning repair point. No exhaustive search proves such a point cannot exist. Prior [runtime evidence](ApplicableSceneOperations.md#broken-marker-preflight) showed the tested `OnWorldLoad` repair after the warning, and separately demonstrated selective current-message removal in one collector.

## Origin and placement

`Helpers.WorldTransformer.OnEnable` resets its offset to zero and starts its recurring check. `CurrentTarget` converts `CameraSelector.shared.CurrentCameraPosition` to tiles; `MoveWorldIfNeeded` schedules a move only when either absolute tile delta exceeds `tileRange`, and `ScheduleMoveWorldDelayed` waits one second before setting the pending move for `FixedUpdate`. `PerformMove` moves registered transforms, updates the offset and publishes `WorldDidMoveEvent` after invoking `OnDidMove`. `GameToWorld(v)` adds the current offset; `WorldToGame(v)` subtracts it. A quiet interval is not a native readiness guarantee, and a subsequent camera move can cause another shift. `TryGetShared(out WorldTransformer)` and the instance `OnDidMove` event are available observation points, subject to lifecycle and subscription cleanup.

`Track.TrackRelativePosition.ConfigureAndApply(Transform, Vector3, float, bool)` stores the target and settings and returns the result of `TryApply`, also recording `LastRuntimeApplySucceeded`. `TryApply` needs a marker, its valid `PositionRotation` and a target. `MoveTransform` converts the game position with `GameToWorld`, adds the rotated offset, optionally raycasts to terrain, and writes world position and rotation only on success. A failed terrain raycast returns false before those writes. `TrackMarker.Location` invalidates its cached pose and raises `OnLocationChanged`; it does not itself apply the transform or update graph registration. Logical rebind and physical placement therefore require separate verification.

## Group observation and restoration

`TrackRelativePosition.Awake` finds its marker and, when configured to follow a track group, calls `ObserveTrackGroup`. During play, `TryApply` unsubscribes and observes again after its placement attempt, even when that attempt fails; an absent marker returns earlier. `ObserveTrackGroup` returns without subscribing if the location or segment is absent. Otherwise it records the segment group, subscribes to `Graph.GroupEnabledDidChange` for a nonempty group when a graph exists, and updates the target active state from `Graph.enabledGroupIds`; an empty group activates the target. `FollowTrackGroup(Action<bool>)` stores a callback and enables following but does not itself perform observation. `OnDestroy` unsubscribes; no `OnDisable` method exists in this type. The subscriber uses the current `Graph.Shared` on unsubscribe, so a graph replacement requires particular care rather than assuming detachment from the old graph. These are static paths, not proof that a particular scene instance changes groups correctly or that restored settings restore subscriptions.

A minimal later disposable-fixture probe can record marker identity, `Graph.Shared`, old and new group IDs and enabled states, target active state, and callbacks while toggling old and new groups independently before rebind, after `ConfigureAndApply`, and after restoration. Use a nonzero offset and angle, record success, local/world/game poses and target hierarchy; include a separately controlled failed terrain raycast. Confirm old-group toggles no longer affect the target and new-group toggles do, then restore the original binding, placement configuration and group states and repeat both toggles. Observe origin via `WorldToGame(Vector3.zero)` and `WorldDidMoveEvent`; reject a comparison spanning a shift unless coordinates are converted consistently. Do not infer detach/restore from pose alone.

For the earlier callback question, a separate read-only probe can subscribe to `MapWillLoadEvent` from a lifecycle point proved to precede launch, then record event order, unique target count, graph identity and readiness, and marker warning timing through a fresh editor and ordinary-save load. It must distinguish no target, no graph, unresolved destination and duplicate target. Only if all required state is available before `TrackMarker.OnEnable` can a later controlled mutation test the proposed prevention route. These experiments are proposals, not observations or proof of a supported hook.
