## Limits and follow-up

No new, independently verified post-0.6 runtime log family beyond the previously identified track-marker lead was found in accessible Nexus posts. Do not infer a broken object or a causal editor defect from a mod description. Search-index copies are primary Nexus text but may be stale or incomplete; direct post endpoints returned 403 for #2040, #2003, #1947 and #1937. The #2062 post endpoint was inaccessible without an HTTP status supplied by the web tool. No authentication bypass was attempted. Obtain the relevant posts through the user's normal browser and the author-distributed archives through normal download channels.

The official [0.6.0 announcement](https://community.railroadergame.com/t/railroader-0-6-0-experimental-is-now-available/147) says old saves and existing mods are incompatible with the experimental release. The [official 0.6.0 release notes](https://docs.railroadergame.com/release-notes/0-6-0/) describe the map editor as work in progress and identify major base-map realignments. The [0.6.2 notes](https://community.railroadergame.com/t/railroader-0-6-2-experimental-release-notes/285) record an identifier validation indicator and a change to interchange internals; neither establishes a particular mod defect.

Nexus listings for #2062, #2003 and #1947 require author permission for modified files and prohibit uploading the files to other sites. Keep archive analysis and any later sandbox test local; seek permission before redistribution or publishing modified assets. No archives were downloaded, mods installed, game run or saves touched for this research.
